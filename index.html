Animal Theory Uploader

Animal Theory Uploader is a personal automation tool used by the owner of the Animal_Theory YouTube channel to publish their own videos on a schedule.

The app takes videos that the channel owner has placed in their own Google Drive folder and uploads them to their own YouTube channel automatically. It is used only by the channel owner for their own content.

For details on how data is handled, see our Privacy Policy.
