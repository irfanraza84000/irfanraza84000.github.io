Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Animal Theory Uploader ("the app") handles information. The app is a personal tool used solely by the owner of the Animal_Theory YouTube channel to upload their own videos.

Information the app accesses

  • Google Drive (read-only): the app reads video files from a specific Google Drive folder chosen by the owner, only to download them for upload.
  • YouTube (upload): the app uploads those videos to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

How information is used

Access is used exclusively to move the owner's own videos from their Google Drive to their own YouTube channel. The app does not access, collect, or process data belonging to any other person.

Data sharing and storage

The app does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties. No personal data is stored beyond the minimal records (which video was uploaded) needed to avoid duplicate uploads. Video files are downloaded temporarily during upload and are not retained.

Data retention

Authorization tokens are stored securely and used only to perform uploads on the owner's behalf. Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions.
